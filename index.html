Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate price per unit?
Divide the total price by the quantity. The product with the lower price per unit is the better deal.
Does this tool work offline?
Yes. After the first load, the entire app runs in your browser without an internet connection.
Which units are supported?
Mass: g, kg, oz, lb. Volume: ml, L. Plus a generic count unit.
Is my data private?
Yes. Everything runs locally. No data is sent to any server, no account required.
